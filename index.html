The changing cost of building a computer

Is AI making PC building more expensive?

The rapid growth of AI is creating huge demand for data-centre hardware. That demand is putting pressure on the same global chip supply chain that helps produce the memory and graphics cards used in gaming PCs.

A bigger AI industry can mean a bigger PC build bill.

AI companies are buying enormous amounts of specialised processors and memory for their data centres. Manufacturers are prioritising some of these high-value products, leaving less flexibility in the wider memory market. When supply is tight, prices can rise for PC builders too. [1]

Parts caught in the price pressure

Why should PC builders care?

01

Higher component costs

RAM, SSDs and graphics cards can take a larger share of a PC budget, making a planned build more expensive.

02

Harder choices

Builders may need to compare more carefully, choose different parts or delay an upgrade to stay within budget.

Important: AI demand is a major pressure on parts of the memory market, but it is not the only reason prices change. Retail pricing is also affected by supply, manufacturing costs, product launches, exchange rates and local stock.
